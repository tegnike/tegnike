# Hi there 👋

English | [日本語](https://github.com/tegnike/tegnike/blob/main/README.ja.md)

I'm Nike, an engineer who builds AI agents and AI characters. I live in Poland and work fully remotely, mostly with companies in Japan.

I started as a web engineer in 2018, working mainly on Ruby on Rails backends along with frontend and infrastructure. Since 2024 my work has centered on LLM-based development.

## What I work on

- **AI agents** — on a business automation service where people hand tasks to AI, I implemented the flow in which the AI drafts a plan and runs it after a person approves. I also worked on a service where AI scores and summarizes articles and turns them into audio
- **AI characters** — long-term memory with vector search, speech synthesis, VRM avatars with lip sync and emotion-driven expressions
- **Evaluating AI coding agents** — having agents generate code and design documents, then scoring the output by test pass rate and difference from reference answers

## Open source

### [AITuberKit](https://github.com/tegnike/aituber-kit)

A toolkit for building AI character chat and AITuber streams, written in TypeScript, React, and Next.js. Public since October 2023. ([demo](https://aituberkit.com), [docs](https://docs.aituberkit.com))

- 1,100+ stars, 230+ forks, 120+ releases, with changes merged from about 30 outside contributors
- Supports many LLM and TTS services and both 2D and 3D character models
- I own the product: specifications, releases, reviews of outside contributions, and support
- Also available under a commercial license, with paid priority development for companies
- Introduced in the book "AITuberを作ってみたらプロンプトエンジニアリングがよくわかった件" (Nikkei BP, 2024) and in articles by MoguLive and WEEL

### Other repositories

- [Japanese-RP-Bench](https://github.com/tegnike/Japanese-RP-Bench) — a benchmark for the role-play performance of Japanese LLMs. Version 2 measures character adherence, long-term stability, resistance to persona replacement, and recovery after failure ([dashboard](https://japanese-rp-bench.tegnike.chatgpt.site))
- [AITuberList](https://github.com/tegnike/aituber-list) — a directory of AITubers on YouTube ([site](https://aituberlist.net))
- [deepseek-chan-vrm](https://github.com/tegnike/deepseek-chan-vrm) — a VRM 1.0 character model
### AI Nike-chan

I develop and operate [AI Nike-chan](https://nikechan.com/about?lang=en), an AI character active on X, Discord, and YouTube since 2023. Her memory and speech systems are built in-house: original messages are stored, turned into per-person notes and vector-searchable memories, and retrieved to match the person and topic when she replies.
